termie

your terminal, pocket-sized.

in a terminal on your computer:

$ curl -fsSL https://mxkkyexe.github.io/termie/install.sh | sh

then scan the qr code with termie.

you'll need

  • tailscale on the computer and your phone

  • ssh turned on · termie checks the rest

  • wsl, with tailscale and ssh inside it
